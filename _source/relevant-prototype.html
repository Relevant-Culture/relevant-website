WHAT WE DO

Building relevant narratives for contemporary culture.

We enable leading cultural institutions in Europe to outsource the management of tailor-made exhibitions and programmes.

01

Advise & position

We support the positioning of cultural institutions, working with public foundations and private initiatives on exhibition project management and program design.

02

Curate & plan

We curate programs and exhibitions and plan the journey — translating contemporary topics at the intersection of social change, science, arts and technology into cohesive stories.

03

Produce & deliver

Mostly through hands-on management of exhibitions, experiences and mediation events, we partner with institutions to bring their mission to life.
